# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** August 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Mezgebe Sbhat** ("the App", "we", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the App. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about what happens with your data.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Information You Provide

The App does **not** require you to create an account, register, or provide any personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

### 1.2 Information Stored on Your Device

To provide its features, the App stores certain information **locally on your device**. This data never leaves your device:

| Data | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Recently opened sections | To show your recent history and make navigation faster |
| Last played song and playback position | To resume playback where you left off |
| Favorites, bookmarks, and playlists | To maintain your personal library and saved content |
| Spiritual routine schedule and settings | To send the prayer/reminder notifications you configured |
| Language preference (e.g., Amharic/English) | To display the App in your preferred language |
| Theme preference (light/dark) | To keep your selected appearance |
| Downloaded audio and PDF files | To let you listen to hymns and read books offline |

This information is stored using your device's local storage (e.g., `SharedPreferences` and the app's private document directory). It is **not** transmitted to us or to any server.

### 1.3 Information Collected Automatically

The App does **not** collect any analytics, usage statistics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, or device identifiers.

The App does **not** use tracking cookies, third-party analytics (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics), crash reporting services, or advertising SDKs.

---

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the locally stored information described above **only** to:

- Provide and improve the App's core functionality (playback, downloads, bookmarks, playlists);
- Remember your preferences (language, theme, and routine schedule);
- Resume your last played content;
- Deliver the notifications you have requested.

We do **not** use your information for advertising, profiling, or any purpose not described in this policy.

---

## 3. Content Downloads and Network Access

To play audio and display books, the App may:

- **Download audio and PDF files** from remote content servers so you can use them offline.
- **Stream audio** during playback.

When the App downloads or streams content, your device's IP address and basic request information are visible to the hosting servers, as is typical for any internet request. This is limited to delivering the content you requested and is not used by us to identify or track you.

---

## 4. Notifications

With your permission, the App sends local notifications (for example, prayer and hymn-checkup reminders based on the spiritual routine you set up). On Android, the App may request permission to schedule "exact" alarms so reminders appear at the precise time you chose. These notifications are generated **on your device** and do not involve sending your data to us.

You can revoke notification permission at any time through your device settings or by turning off notifications in the App.

---

## 5. Permissions Requested

The App may request the following permissions, used only for the purposes stated:

| Permission | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Internet | To download and stream audio and PDF content |
| Notifications | To show the reminders and playback notifications you configured |
| Exact alarm (Android) | To schedule reminders at your chosen time |

We do not request access to your contacts, location, camera, or microphone.

---

## 6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do **not** sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties.

The only third-party services involved are the content hosting servers used to deliver the audio and PDF files you request (see Section 3), and the standard platform services (Google Play / Apple App Store) that distribute the App. These parties process data only as necessary to provide the service, and we do not receive or retain any personal data from you as a result.

---

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your device:

- Data is retained for as long as you keep the App installed and do not delete it.
- To delete all App data, uninstall the App from your device or clear the App's storage from your device settings. This permanently removes your favorites, bookmarks, playlists, preferences, and downloaded content.
- We do not store any of your data on our servers, so there is nothing for us to delete.

---

## 8. Children's Privacy

The App is a spiritual and religious application and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the App does not collect personal information at all, no special parental consent is required beyond standard app-store age ratings.

---

## 9. Data Security

Your data is stored in the protected, private storage area of your device and is not transmitted to us. We follow reasonable security practices and rely on the security measures provided by your device's operating system to protect locally stored data.

Please note that no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
